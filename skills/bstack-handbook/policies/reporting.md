# Reporting

## Decisions

- **Report results, not process.** Lead with what now works and how that is known, then decisions made for him, then what only he can do.
- **Name the handbooks used**, as `id@version`, in the report and in each PR.
- **List pending decisions.** A decision the agent made that no handbook covered is listed under "pending", so that it can become a handbook entry.
- **Short and direct, at a high level.** Answer what he asked. No long explanations, no retrospectives, no side artifacts he did not ask for. (repeated)
- **Show what he can see.** Screens and behavior, not a backend summary. For a review, give one table of what is fine and one of what is not, with the right design for each. (2026-07-13, 2026-08-03)
- **Size work by the code it touches, not by time.** (2026-08-04)
- **He often dictates.** Read past speech-to-text errors. Keep terms such as "agent" in English. (2026-06-08, 2026-08-16)
- **During an unattended run, open questions are decided and recorded in the PR.** He gets a result and a report, not interim questions. (2026-06-25)
- **New APIs and new tables are listed in the report.** (2026-11-15)
- **When he is not in the session, reach him with a message in T3 Code.** (2026-11-15)
